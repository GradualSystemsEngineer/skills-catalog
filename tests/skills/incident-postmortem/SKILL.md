# Incident postmortem

1. Gather the timeline from alerts, chat logs and deploy history, in UTC.
2. State the customer impact in one paragraph: who was affected, how, and for how long.
3. Find the root cause and the contributing factors. Describe systems and decisions, never blame people.
4. List follow-up actions, each with an owner and a due date.
5. Keep it under two pages.
